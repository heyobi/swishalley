## r/iPhoneDuo

**Title:** I made an arcade basketball game for iPhone Duo where the hoop stands on the fold (and pops out in 3D if you want)

**Body:**

Stand your Duo half open like a tiny laptop: the upright half is the backboard, the lower half is the floor with
a ball rack, and the rim hangs right above the crease. Flick the ball up to shoot; flick speed is power, direction
is aim. 60-second rounds, three in a row puts you "on fire", and the last 10 seconds count double while the hoop
starts to slide.

The thing I cared most about: the geometry. The game reads the live hinge angle (not just open/closed) and the real
fold position from iOS, so at 90°, 110° or 130° the backboard still looks upright, the floor still looks flat,
and the ball's arc follows the real shape of the phone. You can fold it mid-shot and the court follows. If the top
half leans in past 90°, the glass actually becomes a wall the ball bounces off.

Optional head tracking: the front camera finds your eyes and each half is drawn from your point of view, so the
hoop seems to stand out of the phone and you can lean to look around the backboard. Frames never leave the phone,
and the game plays exactly the same with it off.

Open it flat for a face-to-face duel: one half each, the fold is the centre line. Race at the same time or take
turns. The duel is free, forever, no ads.

Pro is a one-time purchase for extra arenas, balls and two modes. No subscription, no ads, no account.
