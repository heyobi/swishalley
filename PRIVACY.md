# Swish Alley privacy policy

Swish Alley does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- **Camera (optional).** If you turn on head tracking ("Follow my eyes"), the front camera is used to find where your eyes are, so the hoop can be drawn from your point of view. Each frame is analysed on your device by Apple's Vision framework and discarded immediately. No image, video, face data or measurement is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time; the game plays the same without it.
- Music and sounds are synthesized on your device.
- Your scores, personal bests and choices (arena, ball, music) are stored only on your device.
- Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Swish Alley only receives whether Swish Alley Pro is unlocked.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/swishalley/issues
