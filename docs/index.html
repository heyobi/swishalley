Questions

Is there a basketball game for iPhone Duo?

Swish Alley stands the backboard on the upright half of a half-open iPhone Duo and lays the floor on the lower half, using the live hinge angle and the real fold position, so the hoop looks right at any angle.

Can two people play basketball on one phone?

Yes. Open iPhone Duo flat between you (or use any iPhone or iPad) and each player gets one half. Race at the same time or take turns. The duel is free.

How does the 3D hoop work?

Each half of the display is rendered with an off-axis projection from your eye position, found by the front camera. The hoop stays fixed in space as you move your head. Head tracking is optional.

Does Swish Alley work offline and without ads?

Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed.

What does Swish Alley Pro add?

Three more arenas, four more balls and Wild Toss, where you throw watermelons, rubber ducks, toasters and pumpkins, for one payment. No subscription. Every mode, the duel included, is free.
